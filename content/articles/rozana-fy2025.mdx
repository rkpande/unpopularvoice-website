## The Audit That Documents a Wind-Down

Rozana's FY2025 audit is short. The Statement of Profit and Loss has zero revenue.

Domestic turnover: zero. Export turnover: zero. Sale of goods manufactured: zero. Sale of goods traded: zero. Sale or supply of services: zero.

The only income line that has a number is "Interest income" (₹12.17 Lakhs) and "Other non-operating income" (₹12.04 Lakhs).

The only expense lines that have numbers are auditor fees (₹0.50 Lakhs) and other expenses (₹0.10 Lakhs). Total expenses: ₹0.60 Lakhs.

The number of employees on the closure of the financial year, by direct disclosure: zero.

This is not a company in early stage. This is a company whose operating business is gone.

## What the Directors' Report Says

The Board's report attached to the annual filing is direct:

> "During FY 25, the Company had no revenue from operations. The net profit after tax for the current accounting year is INR 17.47 Lakhs (Indian Rupees Seventeen Point Four Seven Lakhs only). The management is actively evaluating new business opportunities and potential projects/ventures to commence operations in the near future."

There is no obfuscation. The report does not claim the business is in transition or restructuring. It states there was no operating revenue, the profit is the post-tax interest income, and management is looking for what to do next.

The same report confirms: "During FY 25 and as on the date of this report, the Company has no employees. Accordingly, under the said Act, the Company is not required to constitute an Internal Committee."

## What's Not Visible in This Filing

A standalone audit captures one legal entity. It does not necessarily capture the brand's full operating footprint.

The legal entity Rozana Social Commerce Private Limited (CIN U51909DL2021PTC379426, Delhi, 2021) shows the situation described above. The brand "Rozana" may have operated, or may continue to operate, through different entities, group structures, or partnerships not within the scope of this audit.

The original venture funding (publicly reported as approximately $22.5M Series A in 2022 from B Capital, Aroa Ventures, and Lightspeed) is not visible in the current balance sheet of this entity. The face value of equity share capital is ₹1.03 Lakhs against 10,309 shares; net worth is ₹1.78 Cr. This is incompatible with the level of capital reportedly raised. Either the funding was deployed through a different entity, or it was deployed and lost during the operating phase, or both.

What this filing does establish is that, as of March 31, 2025, the legal entity registered to operate the Rozana brand had no revenue, no employees, and no active operating business. Whether that situation changed in FY2026 is a separate question that future filings will answer.
